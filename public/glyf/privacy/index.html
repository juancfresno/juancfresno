Privacy Policy

GLYF does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. Everything you create lives on your device. No accounts, no servers, no analytics.

01 — CAMERA

GLYF uses your camera exclusively to render the AR experience and to capture photos and videos when you press the shutter. The camera feed is processed on-device in real time and is never recorded, stored or transmitted without your action.

02 — YOUR CAPTURES

Photos and videos you capture are saved only to your device's photo library. Sharing happens through the native iOS share sheet — GLYF never uploads your content anywhere.

03 — NO ACCOUNTS

GLYF requires no sign-up, email or identification of any kind. There is no profile of you on any server, because there are no servers.

04 — NO THIRD PARTIES

The app includes no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. No information is shared with third parties, because no information is collected.

05 — CONTACT
